# 🚚 Supply Chain Performance & Risk Analytics

> An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze supply chain performance, financial metrics, supplier activity, product performance, quality risk, lead-time risk, and operational efficiency.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

**Supply Chain Performance & Risk Analytics** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The project transforms supply chain data into interactive business insights using:

- Data preparation and cleaning
- Power Query transformations
- DAX-based calculations
- KPI development
- Interactive slicers
- Business-oriented data visualizations
- Supply chain risk analysis

The dashboard provides a centralized view of:

- Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Total Cost
- Profit Margin
- Product performance
- Location performance
- Supplier performance
- Quality risk
- Lead-time risk
- Average lead time
- Product defect rates

The final Power BI report contains **two analytical pages**:

1. **Supply Chain Performance**
2. **Risk & Quality Analysis**

# 🧮 DAX Measures

The dashboard uses DAX measures to calculate important business metrics.

## 💰 Total Revenue

    Total Revenue =
    SUM('Supply_Chain_Performance_Risk_Dataset'[Revenue])

## 💵 Total Cost

    Total Cost =
    SUM('Supply_Chain_Performance_Risk_Dataset'[Total_Cost])

## 📈 Gross Profit

    Gross Profit =
    [Total Revenue] - [Total Cost]

## 📊 Profit Margin %

    Profit Margin % =
    DIVIDE([Gross Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)

## 📦 Total Orders

    Total Orders =
    DISTINCTCOUNT('Supply_Chain_Performance_Risk_Dataset'[Order_ID])

## ⏱️ Average Lead Time

    Average Lead Time =
    AVERAGE('Supply_Chain_Performance_Risk_Dataset'[Lead_Time_Days])
